🗂️ Poster Breakdown

Poster 1 — "The Last Tree"

Message: Every tree cut is a breath stolen. Every forest lost is a future erased.
Visual Concept: A lush tree glowing against a dark, misty forest background, symbolizing what we stand to lose if deforestation continues.
Call to Action: Plant. Protect. Preserve. — #EarthIsNotDisposable

Poster 2 — "No Trees. No Tomorrow."

Message: Every tree saved protects countless lives.
Visual Concept: A split-screen image contrasting a thriving green forest with a barren, deforested wasteland — a direct visual metaphor for the consequences of inaction.
Call to Action: Join the Movement. Protect Our Forests. — "Protect today. Breathe tomorrow."

Poster 3 — "Grow Green, Grow Life"

Message: One tree planted today creates a greener tomorrow. Together, we can restore what we've lost.
Visual Concept: An illustrated scene of people of different generations planting trees together, representing hope, unity, and collective action.
Call to Action: Plant One Tree Today.

✍️ Design Rationale

This campaign was built around a simple idea: make people feel the loss before they see the statistics. Rather than relying purely on data or facts, each poster uses a strong visual and emotional hook first, paired with a short, memorable headline.


Poster 1 uses a dark, atmospheric background to create urgency and weight around the message — the glowing tree feels precious and fragile rather than just decorative.
Poster 2 is built on contrast — placing a healthy forest directly beside a dead landscape lets the image do the persuading before the text even needs to.
Poster 3 shifts tone deliberately from "warning" to "hope," ending the campaign on a constructive, actionable note — showing people working together rather than highlighting only the problem.


Typography was kept bold and condensed throughout to ensure readability at a distance, which is essential for posters meant for print and outdoor display. The recurring olive/gold accent color was used to tie the posters together as one campaign, even though each poster has its own distinct visual approach.
